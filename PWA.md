# 📲Installation as an App

LinesShines is a PWA — Progressive Web App.

Install once, and it runs like a native app with its own icon.

No browser address bar, and has offline-aware caching for static assets.

---

## 📱iOS

1. Open [lines-shines.com](https://www.lines-shines.com) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button — square with an arrow one.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm — icon lands on your home screen, like any other app.

---

## 💻macOS

**🧭Safari**
1. Open [lines-shines.com](https://www.lines-shines.com).

2. Menu bar → **File → Add to Dock**.
   
   ↳ Or share icon → Add to Dock.

3. LinesShines appears in your Dock as a standalone app.

**🌈Chrome**
1. Open [lines-shines.com](https://www.lines-shines.com).

2. Click **Install** icon on address bar's right side.

   ↳ Or ⋮ menu → Cast, Save, and Share → Install LinesShines.

3. Find it in **Launchpad**, or drag it into your Dock.

---

## 🤖Android

1. Open [lines-shines.com](https://www.lines-shines.com) in Chrome.

2. Tap the **⋮** menu → **Install app**.

   ↳ Tap **Install** if Chrome shows a banner automatically.

3. Confirm — LinesShines is added to your home screen and app drawer.

---

## 🪟Windows

**Edge** — best support
1. Open [lines-shines.com](https://www.lines-shines.com).

2. Click **Install** icon on address bar's right side.

   ↳ Or ⋯ menu → Apps → Install this site as an app.

3. LinesShines opens in its own window and is added to Start Menu.

**🌈Chrome**
1. Open [lines-shines.com](https://www.lines-shines.com).

2. Click **Install** icon on address bar's right side.

   ↳ Or ⋮ menu → Cast, Save, and Share → Install LinesShines.

3. Find it in Start Menu, or pin it to your taskbar.

---

Once installed, LinesShines always pulls live data. **Metrics are never cached.**

So what you see is the latest weekly update straight from PFF Premium Stats.
