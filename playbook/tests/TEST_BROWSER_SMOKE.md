# test_browser_smoke.py

> **Add tests here when:** changing how the controls are wired to the Apply gate (INVARIANTS 4), to the Pinned Players invalidation and its confirm dialog (9), to the Merge Card's display, 5-player cap and 8-player quota (10), to the Linemate roster and its Line Summary (11), to the cards' immunity to axes, toggles, folding and dragging (16), or to the commit points that re-check selected Players (17).

- **Kind:** browser (Playwright), real clicks, typing and a mouse drag on the real page at 1280x900. The pure rules are tested elsewhere (`TEST_PINNED_IMPACT.md`, `TEST_MERGE_TABLE.md`); these tests check that the controls reach them.
- **Needs:** Chromium; the tests skip without it. Its own `seeded_smoke_db` (ten edge rushers at 400 down to 235 opportunities, two interior linemen, 2025, no PFF data) replaces the shared rows, and its `page` fixture opens the app at the default bar of 230.
- **Covers:**
  - **4:** position and bar edits change nothing on the chart until Apply; the Players search follows the pending position while the chart and the applied filters stay put.
  - **17:** dragging the slider or typing a number removes nobody and shows no toast; releasing the slider, leaving the number box, and Enter in the number box (which runs Apply) remove the players who no longer clear the bar and show the toast; a removed player is not restored when the bar moves back.
  - **9:** a higher bar removes only the pinned players who no longer fit, a Comparison with two members left shrinks and one with one left dissolves, and the confirm dialog lists both; Cancel changes nothing; a position change clears everything; a looser bar needs no confirm.
  - **10:** a Comparison's metric cells show percentiles only (ordinals, or a dash for a metric with no data) and no `#rank/N`; the Create popup refuses a sixth player; picking players never checks the Workspace quota, Submit does (10 distinct players against 8).
  - **16:** with a Comparison, a Player Card and a Linemate Card open, changing both axes (then Apply, which needs no confirm), the Player Names, Team Logos and Include metric notes toggles, folding and unfolding a card, and dragging a card each leave the Pinned panel, the quota and the set of open cards exactly as they were.
  - **11:** the Linemate roster of a MIA anchor has his teammates only, in snap order, across both positions; ticking "Include anchor" inserts him in snap order and a tie keeps the teammate first (title changes to "& His Linemates"); unticking restores the list; the Line Summary's RSWA for Win Rate equals a by-hand snap-weighted mean of the percentiles (54th, where a plain mean gives 60th), with and without the anchor.
- **Not here:** the single-function rules → `TEST_PINNED_IMPACT.md`; the Merge Card's layouts → `TEST_MERGE_TABLE.md`; the union of Teams and Players → `TEST_FILTERS.md`.
- **Known gaps:** the Set Threshold lane of INVARIANTS 17 is not driven (it only lowers the bar, so it cannot remove anyone and a test could not tell it from doing nothing); the Apply lane is reached through Enter in the number box, because a click on Apply first leaves the field and so also fires the number change. A category change is not clicked through (the reset outcome is covered in `TEST_PINNED_IMPACT.md`). The slider drag aims at the thumb's expected position and relies on a drag past the end clamping to the maximum; a real touch drag is not tested. The 5-player cap is checked only in the Create popup, not in the Edit popup. The 8-player quota in the Single Cards add box is not covered. For 16, the drag test only proves the card moved and the Workspace did not change, not where it landed; the Edit popup and the Comparison's own fold are not driven. For 11, "uncapped" is shown against a cap of 300 snaps (the seed goes up to 380) and not against larger caps; the Career roster and its name are in `TEST_NCAAF_CAREER_PAGE.md`, and the mixed ED and DI roster uses the seeded BUF and MIA teams only.
