# test_ncaaf_career_page.py

> **Add tests here when:** changing the NCAAF Career page: the Status Filter, the two threshold bars (Joined NFL and Potential Draftees), how the pool and the highlight follow them, or how the Season pages differ from it.

- **Kind:** browser (Playwright / Chromium), against the live test server.
- **Needs:** `live_server_url` from `conftest.py`, the file's own `seeded_career_page_db` (four members of one position: two Joined NFL, two Potential Draftees, set around the default bars read from `config`) and, for the Season-page check, `seeded_ncaaf_db`.
- **Covers:** the Career page shows the Status dropdown (a button like Teams, closed at first) and both bars and hides Season; only Potential Draftees is lit at first; neither bar is ever greyed or tied to the Status Filter, and both stay editable whatever is lit; the Status button's label follows the ticks (All Status, one name, No Status); the bars start at the default of their own status; each player must clear the bar of his own status; lighting Joined NFL only changes the dimming and leaves the pool as it was; a bar of a status that is off still counts toward the pool; raising one bar changes only its status's players; the Career page starts with No Teams, so only the lit status is highlighted (read from the label opacity of the plot); lighting a status, choosing a team or picking a player each highlight their players (the union), and the pool stays the same; the chart title names Career and the subtitle reports a count per status on its own line (Potential Draftees first, then Joined NFL); a changed bar lights Apply; the Season pages keep one bar, no Status Filter and no extra applied state; the NFL Alumni card — its roster keeps only Joined NFL members of the same school and line who clear the Joined NFL bar (a Potential Draftee and a below-bar Joined NFL member are both left out), and the Player Card button and card title read "NFL Alumni"; every card marks the player's status (the Player Card's Metadata, the NFL Alumni card's and the Comparison Card's Status rows), the Player Card writes the threshold as one line per status, the Comparison Card writes both bars with their status, and the NFL Alumni card states only the Joined NFL bar.
- **Not here:** the rule itself (a threshold is a number or a pair, who is eligible) -> `TEST_PINNED_IMPACT.md`; the Career routes and the merge -> `TEST_CAREER_MERGE.md`; the page's served markup -> `TEST_APP_STARTUP.md`.
- **Known gaps:** the opacity of the logos and dots (only the labels' is read back); the conference buttons of the Teams checklist on Career; Pinned Players, cards and the Below Threshold popup on Career; the layout of the two bars on a phone (checked by hand).
